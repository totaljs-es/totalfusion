Sends a message for every event of the workers that you choose: `worker:ready`, `worker:exit`, `worker:recycle`, `worker:unresponsive`, `call:retry` and `reload`. The message has the `event`, when it `at` happened and the data of the event (`runtime`, `pid`, `reason`...). Use it to warn somebody when a worker stops answering or to keep a log.
